How It Works

Here's what actually happens.

Six steps, written out in full so there are no surprises. Most people are through the first three inside a fortnight.

1
Step one

You reach out

Fill a short form telling us about your situation: who you are, what you have done, and what has been going wrong. Plain language is fine. You do not need a polished CV to start.

2
Step two

You book a call

Once we understand your needs, we send a scheduling link so you can pick a time that works. Evenings and weekends are available, because most of the people we help are still working.

3
Step three

Assessment

You speak with an advisor who understands where you are. This is a conversation, not a test. We are working out what is genuinely in the way, which is often not what the job boards suggest.

4
Step four

We identify how to help

Advice, mentoring, CV help, or connecting you to opportunities. Sometimes it is all four; often it is one thing done properly. We tell you which, and why.

5
Step five

We take action

We may coach you, introduce you through our network, or reach out to hiring managers on your behalf. This is the part that makes us different from advice: we will make the approach ourselves.

6
Step six

We stay in touch

Including a personal conversation at the end, whatever the outcome. If it worked, we want to hear about it. If it did not, we want to know what we missed.

What we ask of you

Honesty and engagement.

Tell us the real situation, turn up to the call, and follow through on what you agree to. That is the whole of it.
