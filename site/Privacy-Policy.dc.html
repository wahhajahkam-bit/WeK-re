Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026

1. Introduction

1.1. We Käre ("we," "us," "our") is a pro bono, light-touch service that helps job seekers — including those transitioning roles, returning after a gap, new to the region, retired professionals, or recent graduates — find their next opportunity. This Policy explains what personal data we collect through the Site and related service (together, "the Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights regarding that data.

1.2. This Policy applies to all candidates, mentors, visitors, and other individuals who engage with the We Käre Service.

1.3. This Policy should be read alongside the We Käre Terms & Conditions.

1.4. Because our Service involves direct support of your job search, including outreach to third parties on your behalf, we handle more detailed personal data than a typical informational website, and we are committed to doing so carefully, transparently, and only with appropriate safeguards.

2. Scope

2.1. This Policy covers the collection, use, sharing, retention, and protection of personal data gathered through the Service, including:

  • Identity and contact details submitted via the intake / "Get Help" form
  • Career information shared during intake or assessment calls
  • Call recordings of assessment and advisory calls
  • Referral information used to reach out to hiring managers or professional contacts on a candidate's behalf
  • Success story information, shared only with explicit, separate consent
  • Usage and analytics data collected automatically through the Site

2.2. We Käre does not charge for this service and does not collect payment or financial account information.

3. Document Control and Distribution

3.1. This Policy is published on the Site and made available to all candidates and visitors.

3.2. This Policy shall be reviewed periodically, and at minimum annually, and updated as needed to reflect changes in the Service's features, data practices, or applicable law.

3.3. The version published on the Site at any given time is the controlling version, and supersedes any prior version.

4. Information We Collect

4.1. Identity and contact details — your name, email address, phone number, and other contact information you provide through the intake form or during correspondence.

4.2. Career information — your job history, current employment situation, career goals, target roles, and related background, as shared via the intake form or during an assessment call.

4.3. Call recordings — assessment and advisory calls with mentors may be recorded and saved for quality and continuity purposes.

4.4. Referral information — job listings or roles you are interested in, and details used to reach out to hiring managers or facilitate introductions to relevant professional connections on your behalf.

4.5. Success story information — with your explicit, separate consent, your name, photograph, and story details, if you agree to be featured as a testimonial on the Site.

4.6. Usage data — standard technical information collected automatically through the Site, such as pages visited and browser type, for analytics purposes.

5. How We Use Information

5.1. We use the personal data described above for the following purposes:

  • To assess your situation on an intake or advisory call with a mentor
  • To match you with relevant support, guidance, and next steps
  • To contact hiring managers on your behalf regarding specific roles, with your knowledge
  • To facilitate introductions to relevant professional connections, where agreed
  • To track and improve how many candidates we are able to help, for internal reporting purposes
  • To feature your success story publicly, but only with your explicit, separate consent — this is never assumed by default
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of the Site

6. Sharing of Information

6.1. Because this Service involves outreach on your behalf, we share limited information with:

  • Hiring managers, when reaching out about specific roles you have expressed interest in
  • Mentors and advisors supporting your assessment, guidance, and case handling
  • Service providers who help us operate the Site and manage submissions, under confidentiality obligations

6.2. We Käre does not sell your personal data, and does not share your information beyond what is needed to provide the Service, except with your consent or as required by law.

7. Call Recordings

7.1. Assessment and advisory calls may be recorded for quality, continuity, and follow-up purposes.

7.2. Recordings are stored securely and accessed only by those directly supporting your case.

7.3. You may request a copy of, or the deletion of, any call recording involving you, subject to any ongoing case needs.

8. Data Retention

8.1. We retain your information for as long as we are actively supporting your job search, and for a reasonable period afterward in case you return for further support.

8.2. You may request deletion of your data at any time, subject to any legitimate ongoing case needs (see Section 10).

9. Data Security

9.1. We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data, including call recordings and career information, against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

9.2. Access to candidate information is restricted to mentors, advisors, and personnel directly supporting your case.

10. Your Rights

10.1. Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information
  • Withdraw consent for your success story or photo to be featured, at any time
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to any ongoing case needs
  • Request a copy of any call recording involving you
  • Object to specific outreach being made on your behalf

10.2. Requests may be submitted through the contact channels described in Section 13. We will respond to valid requests within a reasonable timeframe.

11. Children's Privacy

11.1. This Service is intended for adults seeking employment and is not directed at individuals under 18.

12. Cookies & Analytics

12.1. The Site may use cookies or similar technologies for basic analytics and functionality. You may control cookies through your browser settings.

13. Changes to This Policy

13.1. This Policy may be updated from time to time. The Effective Date on the title page reflects the latest revision.

14. Contact Us

14.1. Questions about this Policy, or requests regarding your data, can be directed to us through the official contact channels published on the Site.
