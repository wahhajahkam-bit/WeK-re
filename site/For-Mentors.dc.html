What mentoring involves

Three things, and nothing hidden.

Assessing and advising

You talk to a candidate about where they are and what should come next. Your read on their field is the thing we cannot manufacture.

A light, flexible commitment

Built around your availability. Some mentors take one conversation a quarter, some take one a month. Both are useful.

Unpaid, explicitly

Pro bono, and we say so plainly. This is about giving back, and nobody in the chain takes a fee.

Who we're looking for

Experienced professionals, across every field.

People willing to spend a little time helping someone find their way. There is no minimum seniority and no sector we would turn away. The candidates come from everywhere, so the mentors need to as well.

If you have hired, been hired, or changed direction yourself, you already know something a candidate does not.
