About Us

Why We Käre exists.

The same network that opens doors for executives can open one for someone who has been shut out. That is the entire idea.

Our mission

To help people the job market overlooks, for free, by combining real advice with real action. Not a charity in the abstract, but a specific promise kept one candidate at a time.

How we're different

We don't just advise, we act: reaching out on candidates' behalf and opening doors through our network. Advice is cheap and widely available. An introduction from someone a hiring manager trusts is not.

Our place in the ecosystem

We Käre sits inside the Konsälidön ecosystem and draws on the same network of leaders, operators and firms. Konsälidön connects people who are already moving; We Käre is the room for people who cannot get in the building.
